# What's new in SyncCam

## 0.6.0
- A new studio look: dark and calm, with frosted-glass controls over the live picture
- Every camera has its own number and colour, on its tile, in the gallery and on its own screen
- See at a glance which cameras are ready, recording, out of sync or in trouble, and how to fix it
- Rig settings show which cameras took each change, and what the others used instead
- Find Wi-Fi cameras on your network automatically, with pairing help for each brand
- Join with a 4-digit code when you can't scan
- iPhones in the browser now keep every photo and video on the iPhone too, with Save to Photos
- Select several takes or files at once (long-press or tap Select) to share, save or delete
- Make bullet-time animations from photo takes
- Daylight and high-contrast themes, and recent rigs on the start screen

## 0.5.0
- A fresh, polished look with a new 3D logo
- Updates now download and install right inside the app
- Get a notification when a new version is ready, even when SyncCam is closed
- A quick tour the first time you open the app
- Step-by-step guides for phones, iPhones, pro cameras and more (tap the ? on the home screen)
- Clearer, simpler wording everywhere
- Give your shots names, like "First dance", by tapping their title

## 0.4.0
- iPhones join with no app and no certificate
- Connect pro cameras from Sony, Canon, Nikon, Fujifilm, Panasonic, Blackmagic, GoPro and more
- Any other camera can be lined up by sound
